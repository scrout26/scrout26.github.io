Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Scrout is an internal reporting tool for social media accounts operated by Team Secret. This page explains what it reads and what happens to it.

What Scrout reads

For an account that has authorised it: the account's display name and username, follower and video counts, and, for each public post, its link, caption, publish time and view, like, comment and share counts.

Scrout does not read private messages, does not read data about the people who watch or follow the account, and does not collect any data about visitors to this website.

Where it goes

The figures are stored in a private Google Sheet used by the Team Secret social media team for performance reporting. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

Figures are kept for as long as they are useful for reporting. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the connected account's settings on the platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). To have stored figures deleted, use the contact below.
