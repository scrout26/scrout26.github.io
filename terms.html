Terms of Service

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Scrout is a private, internal reporting tool operated for the Team Secret social media team. It is not a public service and is only used with social media accounts that Team Secret operates.

Use

An account is connected only when someone who manages it chooses to authorise Scrout through the platform's own approval screen. Scrout reads performance statistics for that account. It never posts, edits, deletes, comments or sends messages.

Disconnecting

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the connected account's settings on the platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Once withdrawn, Scrout can no longer read any data from that account.

No warranty

Scrout is provided as is, for internal reporting only. Figures come from the platforms' own APIs and may differ from what the platforms show in their apps.
